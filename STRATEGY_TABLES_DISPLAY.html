3 · Cross-asset ETF book — WITH vs WITHOUT leverage

YearUnlevered (1× cash-secured)Levered (10%-vol target)
How the leverage works. Fully cash-secured (1×), the combined book is extremely low-volatility — deep-OTM premium trickles in against reserved cash. The backtest scales positions so portfolio volatility = 10%/yr; because sleeves are near-uncorrelated, the diversified book's raw vol is tiny and the implied gross notional is several × capital (computed above from the actual series). Practically this means selling puts on notional ≈ L× your equity under portfolio margin. All headline numbers (CAGR 25.3%, DD −12.2%) are the levered series; the unlevered column is what the same trades earn with no margin. Crash risk scales with L — the −12% DD assumes 2016–25's worst; a deeper co-crash would exceed it linearly in L.
