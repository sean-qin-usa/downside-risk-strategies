GEFCom2014 — Global Energy Forecasting Competition 2014 (probabilistic tracks)
=============================================================================
WHERE TO GET IT (free):
 - Primary: supplementary data for Hong, Pinson, Fan et al. (2016),
   "Probabilistic energy forecasting: Global Energy Forecasting Competition 2014 and beyond,"
   International Journal of Forecasting 32(3). The data zip is in the article's
   supplementary material on ScienceDirect.
 - Mirror: Dr. Tao Hong's site (search "GEFCom2014 data drhongtao") and several
   public GitHub repos host "GEFCom2014 Data.zip".

WHAT TO DOWNLOAD:
 The competition has 4 probabilistic tracks — download whichever you want to score:
   L  = electricity LOAD          (flagship benchmark: QRA / quantile regression)
   P  = electricity PRICE         (flagship: QRA)
   W  = WIND power                (flagship: quantile regression)
   S  = SOLAR power               (flagship: quantile regression)

HOW TO PLACE IT:
 Unzip so that the per-track CSVs land directly in this folder, e.g.:
   competitions/gefcom2014/Load/ ...
   competitions/gefcom2014/Price/ ...
   competitions/gefcom2014/Wind/ ...
   competitions/gefcom2014/Solar/ ...
 (Exact subfolder names don't matter — I'll auto-detect *.csv here.)

Scoring metric the competition used: PINBALL loss averaged over the 99 quantiles
(tau = 0.01 ... 0.99). I'll build the harness to reproduce that exactly.
