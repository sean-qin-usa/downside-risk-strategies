\subsection{A unit-matched parametric audit (added July 2026)}
\label{sec:samerows}

An earlier internal comparison showed the network beating a $t$-GARCH
benchmark by roughly 38\%; an audit found the two models had been scored
on different universes and possibly different target scalings, and that
figure is retired. The corrected comparison scores both models on
identical (ticker, date, horizon) rows --- 102{,}391 rows, 111 names,
horizons $h = 5/10/21/42/63$ trading days --- against a unit-matched
GJR-GARCH-$t$ (zero-mean, Student-$t$ innovations, annual walk-forward
refits, $h$-day quantiles by Monte Carlo path simulation).

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\begin{threeparttable}
\caption{Same-rows audit: IQN vs unit-matched GJR-GARCH-$t$, average
pinball loss on identical (ticker, date, horizon) rows.}
\label{tab:samerows}
\begin{tabular}{ccccc}
\toprule
Horizon (days) & IQN & GJR-GARCH-$t$ & ratio & DM $t$ \\
\midrule
5  & 0.01302 & 0.01245 & 1.046 & $+12.3$ \\
10 & 0.01871 & 0.01769 & 1.058 & $+17.2$ \\
21 & 0.02762 & 0.02602 & 1.062 & $+18.1$ \\
42 & 0.03895 & 0.03710 & 1.050 & $+14.0$ \\
63 & 0.04757 & 0.04552 & 1.045 & $+12.3$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\begin{tablenotes}\footnotesize
\item The parametric model wins by 4.5--6.2\% at every horizon, all
strongly significant. The gap is concentrated in the tails (downside
$\tau{=}.05$ $\approx$7\% worse at all horizons; the body $\tau{=}.50$
is nearly tied at 1.02--1.05). Applying the paper's past-only pooled
tail recalibration fixes coverage (5\% breach $0.071$--$0.089 \to
0.051$--$0.064$) and roughly halves the pinball gap at long horizons
(ratios $\to$ 1.029--1.048) --- but GJR-GARCH-$t$ still wins: the
deficiency is not purely a fixable tail-calibration artifact.
\end{tablenotes}
\end{threeparttable}
\end{table}

This audit sharpens rather than undermines the paper's claims: on
single-name daily-to-quarterly equity forecasting the leverage-GARCH is
the honest benchmark and the network does not beat it. The network's
demonstrated advantages are exactly the ones GARCH structurally lacks
--- calibrated full-distribution term structures after the EVT graft,
forecasting unseen assets, and the amortized cross-sectional problem ---
and the trading results of Appendix~A never depended on beating GARCH on
average loss.

\paragraph{Weekly tenor, net of measured half-spread (pre-registered
exhibit, now computed).} A weekly (5--15 DTE) VRP series directly
comparable to the monthly book was built from 178{,}787 puts on 542
names, 2016--2025, same cross-sectional selection (top-10\% VRP). At mid
execution weekly beats monthly at every selection tier (top-10\% Sharpe
3.46 vs 2.38) with structurally truncated tails (worst month $-1.2\%$ vs
$-8.4\%$). The decisive test is net of the full bid/ask half-spread:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
top-10\% VRP & mid Sharpe & bid (net) Sharpe & bid worst mo. &
half-spread eats \\
\midrule
Weekly  & 3.46 & \textbf{2.08} & $-1.4\%$ & 57\% of gross \\
Monthly & 2.38 & 0.51 & $-9.6\%$ & 82\% of gross \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

At the bid, monthly collapses ($2.38 \to 0.51$) while weekly holds
($3.46 \to 2.08$): the half-spread consumes 82\% of monthly's fat
deep-OTM premium but only 57\% of weekly's tighter near-dated premium,
and weekly's tail truncation dominates. Weekly is the better vehicle net
of cost. Caveats: ``bid'' is worst-case full-cross (posting at mid lands
between, weekly $\sim$2--3, monthly $\sim$1--2); liquid names only.

\paragraph{Cross-asset diversification, in/out of sample.} Seven liquid
ETF option sleeves (QQQ, TLT, GLD, USO, UNG, FXE, HYG; average pairwise
correlation 0.05): combined risk-weighted Sharpe 2.33 full-sample, 2.30
in-sample (2016--20), 4.46 out-of-sample (2021--25) --- the
diversification benefit is stable across eras, unlike a VIX-regime gate
(a look-ahead artifact, retired). Per-sleeve Sharpes rotate across eras,
which is precisely why the combination is the object. A 10\%-vol-target
combined book runs CAGR 25.3\% with max drawdown $-12.2\%$.

\paragraph{Earnings exposure priced, no filter needed.} Earnings names
carry richer premium (VRP 0.137 vs 0.078; 186 vs 142 bp per trade) but
not the Sharpe (1.94 vs 1.99); down-weighting earnings $0.5\times$
slightly hurts ($2.38 \to 2.24$). Accept earnings exposure at full
weight as priced.

\paragraph{Forward harness validated.} The production spec replayed
through the live signal harness over 116 months reproduces the strategy
(mid Sharpe 2.22, out-of-sample above in-sample). Monthly and weekly
signal generators now run on schedule, building the parallel
out-of-sample paper record whose limit-fill quality is the one number no
backtest can provide. The harness never places orders.
