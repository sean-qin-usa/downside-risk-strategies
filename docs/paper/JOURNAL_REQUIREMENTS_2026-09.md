# Journal requirements, timelines, and what WE must change before each submission
(compiled 2026-09-04; verify fee/format details on the journal site the week of submission)

The single most important fact: **none of these journals have deadlines.** All operate rolling submission — submit any day, the clock starts then. "Timeline" means review speed, not a due date. Sequential submission only (one journal at a time); simultaneous submission is an ethics violation everywhere on this list.

## Target 1 — Journal of Financial Econometrics (JFEC, Oxford/SoFiE)
- Fee: US$65 (free for SoFiE members — joining SoFiE may cost about the same and add a community; check current dues).
- Length: "typically not exceed 40 double-spaced pages." Our 36 compact pages ≈ 50+ double-spaced → **trim needed**: move FZ details, double-sort table, and parts of the protocol section to the online appendix at submission time (the OUP skin pass).
- Abstract: **≤100 words** (ours is ~190 → must cut roughly in half; keep the three results, drop mechanism clauses).
- Keywords 2–6 + JEL codes (we have both).
- **Replication package required** for empirical papers (policy since Mar 2023): upload code + data for editorial replicability review; exemption for licensed data must be requested in the cover letter (CRSP/Bloomberg — our situation exactly; our public repo + rebuild-from-queries design is the strong answer).
- Submit via ScholarOne (Manuscript Central).
- Typical pace (author-reported norms, not official): desk screen a few weeks; first decision ~3–5 months; realistic submission→acceptance ~9–15 months with one or two revision rounds.

## Target 2 — International Journal of Forecasting (IJF, Elsevier/IIF)
- Fee: none listed in the author guide.
- Format: submit a single PDF initially (LaTeX template encouraged; Word accepted); source files after acceptance; figures EPS/PDF only.
- Replication: methodological detail sufficient to replicate is required; code+data as an online supplement is the norm — again, we are well positioned.
- Selectivity (SciRev author reports): ~30% desk-rejected, ~15% accepted.
- Pace: author-reported first decisions commonly ~4–8 months; total to acceptance often 12–18. Budget accordingly if JFEC declines.
- Re-skin needed before IJF: forecasting-first framing (lead with predictive comparison + registered out-of-era forecast evaluation; FRTB/capital material to a section, not the spine).

## Backup tiers (all rolling; typical first decisions 2–6 months unless noted)
- Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (T&F/ASA) — strong methods fit for the SPA/DM/MCS machinery; length ~45pp double-spaced norm.
- Journal of Econometrics (Elsevier) — fits the score-driven/FZ0 econometrics; slower, thorough.
- Quantitative Finance (T&F) — practitioner-methods fit; faster decisions reported.
- Journal of Risk / Journal of Risk Model Validation (Risk.net) — the FRTB battery + backtesting angle is squarely in scope; fast, applied.
- Journal of Banking & Finance / Journal of Empirical Finance (Elsevier) — applied-finance homes.
- Finance Research Letters — short-format (would require a 2,500-word condensation; keep as emergency outlet).
- Journal of Financial Data Science (PMR) — ML-in-finance practitioner outlet; friendly to the amortization story.
- Preprint now, regardless of target: SSRN + arXiv q-fin.RM the week we submit — priority stamp and citability while under review.

## Pre-submission checklist implied by the above (the "OUP skin" pass)
1. Abstract → ≤100 words.
2. Double-space + 1-inch margins build target; push overflow to the online appendix until ≤~40 pages.
3. Cover letter: fit + replication-exemption request for licensed rows (draft exists: docs/COVER_LETTER_JFEC.md).
4. Fee ready ($65) or SoFiE membership.
5. Suggested referees list (drafted; verify affiliations).
6. SSRN/arXiv posting same week.
