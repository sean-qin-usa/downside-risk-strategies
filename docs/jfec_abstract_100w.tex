\begin{abstract}
A deployable engine --- GARCH scale times an amortized nonparametric
residual quantile, with an extreme-value tail and conformal
recalibration --- and a real-time misspecification score that says
when it beats the parametric standard. One pooled fit replaces
per-asset estimation and prices new listings day one. Exception
counts stay nominal at both regulatory levels through a 2008-crisis
test window. The score concentrates the edge in its top decile
($+2.7\%$, DM 6.5) and replicates on an untouched 2000--2013 holdout
under registered predictions. Where the score is quiet the engine
ties the benchmark --- run it everywhere, read the score as a dial.
\end{abstract}
